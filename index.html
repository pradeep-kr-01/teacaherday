FOR YOU

A Letter, Written With Gratitude

Dear Ma'am,

On Teacher's Day, most students thank a teacher for what she taught in the classroom. I want to thank you for everything you taught me outside of it - patience, discipline, and the quiet confidence that comes from knowing someone believes in you.

You work harder than anyone I know, and you never make it look like a burden. Whether it's your students or your own family, you show up fully, every single time. That's not something that can be taught in any syllabus it's just who you are.

What makes you different is that you never treated me like just another student. You've guided me like a mother would guide her own son - checking in on my choices, correcting me when I needed it, and standing behind me when it mattered. And somehow, on top of all that, you've trusted me the way few people do.

I know how much you mean to your daughter, and now I understand it firsthand too. Thank you for every piece of advice about my career, every moment of patience, and every bit of trust you've placed in me. I don't take any of it for granted.

Happy Teacher's Day, Ma'am. I hope this small page says even a fraction of what I actually feel.

— With respect and gratitude, always
